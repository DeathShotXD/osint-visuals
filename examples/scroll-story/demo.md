[map 20.6,-157.3 zoom=6]
The Hawaiian islands. On **8 August 2023**, a wildfire swept through the town of Lahaina on Maui's north-west coast.

[map 20.88,-156.6 zoom=9.5 bearing=-15]
Lahaina sits on Maui's north-west shore, at the foot of the West Maui Mountains.

[map 20.873,-156.677 zoom=13.5 pitch=45 marker="Lahaina"]
This is where the satellite pictures below were taken: a square about 3.6 km across, centred on the old town.

[compare media/lahaina-2023-07-29.jpg media/lahaina-2023-08-18.jpg labels="29 Jul 2023|18 Aug 2023" credit="NASA HLS (Sentinel-2), 30 m, via NASA GIBS; contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023"]
Scroll on and the divider moves from the picture taken on **29 July** to the one taken on **18 August**, ten days
after the fire swept through the town.

The green patches inland are gone, and the town turns grey and brown.

[image media/lahaina-2023-08-18.jpg alt="Satellite image of Lahaina on 18 August 2023, with brown burned ground inland of the town" credit="NASA HLS (Sentinel-2), 30 m, via NASA GIBS; contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023"]
At 30 metres a pixel, single buildings cannot be seen. For that, newsrooms use commercial imagery under licence.
